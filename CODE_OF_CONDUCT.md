# Code of Conduct

ExperimentLens contributors are expected to keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent while still addressing impact.
- Do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, or publication of private information.
- Respect project scope, security boundaries, and maintainers' moderation decisions.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and without retaliation.

---

ExperimentLens 希望所有参与者保持尊重、建设性与包容。请针对想法和代码讨论，给出可执行的反馈；禁止骚扰、歧视、威胁、不受欢迎的性关注或公布他人隐私。如有行为准则问题，请私下联系仓库所有者。
